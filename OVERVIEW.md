# Frontier Engineering in the Field — Module 8

This repository contains a **written capstone simulation**, based exclusively on Taller Academy's fictional *Meridian Health Services* case. It is **not** a claim of a real client engagement, production implementation, live deployment, or completed stakeholder interviews. Proposed dates, targets, permissions, and process changes are part of the simulation.

## Results of the exercise

The provided case reports **34 days** of average end-to-end credentialing time and **770 minutes** of active work. The derived flow efficiency is **1.57%**. The three largest waits are **11, 7, and 5 days**, totaling 23 days. The recommended pilot prioritizes flow redesign and measurable quality-preserving outcomes rather than deploying AI for its own sake.

The scenario's enumerated wait times plus work time do not fully reconcile with the 34-day reported average. The submission explicitly flags this discrepancy for client verification rather than silently correcting source data.

## Evidence boundaries

- Meridian's organization, constraints and figures come from the **fictional training brief**.
- No private client code, messages, credentials, incidents or telemetry are included.
- The 90-day target and implementation are **proposals, not measured achievements**.
- The original Module 1 doubt could not be verified, so the reflection makes that limitation explicit.
- The decision and security controls are a proposed design subject to local policy, licensing and review.
